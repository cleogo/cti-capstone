## Specs
- Product type: Ceiling-type fire extinguisher
- Agent: HCFC (hydrochlorofluorocarbon)
- Sizes on the masterlist: 10 lb, 20 lb (FIR-001 sizes from the code)
- Mounting: Ceiling type

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A ceiling-mounted automatic extinguisher detects fire with a thermal sensor and activates on its own. It fixes to the ceiling with a bracket. One maker offers ceiling-mounted units filled with HCFC-123. HCFC agents are halocarbons, which the U.S. EPA accepts as halon substitutes.

## Advantages
- An automatic ceiling unit activates in a fire by itself.
- It needs no piping or ducting, so installation is quick.
- Halogenated agents do not conduct electricity and leave no residue when they evaporate.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name says ceiling type only. It does not state how CTI's unit activates.
- U.S. OSHA guidance on fixed gaseous systems warns that some agents stay at hazardous levels after discharge.
- U.S. OSHA says to ventilate a flooded area before people reenter without protective clothing and respirators.
- U.S. OSHA notes that some halogenated hydrocarbons decompose at high temperatures.
- Under the Montreal Protocol, developing countries must cut HCFC consumption to zero from 2030. A small servicing allowance runs to 2040.
