## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 150 (AC180P)
- Capacity: 1,440 Wh
- Rated AC output: 1,800 W
- Surge power: 2,700 W
- Power lifting: 2,700 W
- AC charging input: 1,440 W
- Solar input: 500 W max, 12 to 60 V, 10 A
- Self-consumption: about 15 W
- Total outlets: 11

## Usage
Home brownout backup for a refrigerator, fans, lights, a TV and a router.
It also runs power tools and small cooking appliances within 1,800 W. CTI's
solar guide pairs it with two Sora 220 (PV220) panels in series.

## Advantages
- 1,440 W AC charging, the same as its capacity in watt-hours.
- 11 outlets.

## Cautions
- 1,800 W rated output is not enough for most aircon units with their
  starting surge. Use the recommender to check a specific load.
- Solar input is capped at 500 W and 10 A.
