## Specs
- Product type: Pen light (as named on the masterlist)
- Maker: Not named on the masterlist

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A pen light is a small light with the shape and size of a pen. The masterlist lists it with whistles and signaling devices. It is useful to light a small area, for example a keyhole, a panel or the inside of a bag. U.S. NPS says that lighting can help you find your way at night or signal for help. The masterlist states only the name. It does not state the brightness, the runtime or the battery type of CTI's unit. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- It is small enough to carry in a pocket.
- It can go in an emergency kit. U.S. Ready.gov lists a flashlight and extra batteries in a basic kit.
- Be Ready Utah, a U.S. state preparedness site, says that a repeated series of three flashes calls for help at night.

## Cautions
- A pen light is small. It does not light a large area as a full-size flashlight does.
- The masterlist does not state the battery type or the runtime. Ask CTI sales.
- Keep extra batteries, and test the light before you store it in a kit.
- Do not point the light into the eyes of other people.
