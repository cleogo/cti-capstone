## Specs
- Product type: Portable fire extinguisher
- Agent: HFC-236fa. The FIR-002-10LBS-CO2HFC and FIR-002-20LBS-CO2HFC names state HFC-236fa only.
- Sizes on the masterlist: 5 lb, 10 lb, 20 lb, 50 lb
- Mounting: Wall type with a wall bracket (5 lb) or a wall hook (10 lb, 20 lb). Wheeled type (50 lb). The CO2HFC names state no mounting.

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. HFC-236fa is a colorless, odorless clean agent that discharges as a liquid and flashes to a gas. Halogenated extinguishers typically carry listings for Class A, B and C fires, depending on size. Units under 9.5 lb carry no Class A listing. One maker lists computer rooms, laboratories and document storage rooms as typical uses.

## Advantages
- HFC-236fa does not conduct electricity and leaves no residue.
- It does not cause thermal shock damage to delicate electronics.
- HFCs have zero ozone depletion potential because they contain no chlorine.
- One maker's table rates HFC-236fa as less toxic than Halotron I or Halon 1211.

## Cautions
- The U.S. EPA lists a global warming potential of 9,810 for HFC-236fa.
- The Kigali Amendment adds HFC-236fa to the Montreal Protocol's controlled substances. It requires Parties to cut HFC production and consumption in steps.
- The U.S. EPA SNAP program accepts HFC-236fa streaming agents for non-residential uses only.
- UL sets a minimum room volume for each clean agent extinguisher rating. Larger ratings need larger rooms.
