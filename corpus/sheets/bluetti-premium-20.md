## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 20 (AC2P)
- Capacity: 230 Wh
- Rated AC output: 300 W
- Surge power: 600 W
- Power lifting: 600 W
- AC charging input: 270 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 200 W max, 12 to 28 V, 8.2 A
- Self-consumption: about 12 W
- Total outlets: 5

## Usage
The smallest station in the range. It suits phones, laptops, a router, LED
lights and other loads under 300 W. It charges from a wall socket or from a
solar panel of up to 200 W.

## Advantages
- The lowest capacity and output in the range, so it is the lightest to carry.
- Power lifting runs some resistive loads up to 600 W at reduced power.

## Cautions
- 300 W rated output does not run a refrigerator compressor, an aircon, a
  kettle or a rice cooker.
- The station uses about 12 W itself while the inverter is on. Switch the AC
  output off when it is not in use.
