## Specs
- Product type: Wheeled fire extinguisher
- Agent: AFFF (aqueous film-forming foam)
- Sizes on the masterlist: 50, 100 and 200 lb
- Mounting: wheeled type

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit.
AFFF is a firefighting foam for liquid fuel fires. The foam forms a blanket
over the fuel. The blanket stops flammable vapors from escaping and helps
prevent re-ignition. Class B fires involve flammable liquids such as gasoline,
oil, grease and oil-based paints. Read the label for the fire classes the unit
covers.

## Advantages
- AFFF targets Class B liquid fuel fires.
- The foam blanket holds back fuel vapors and helps stop the fire from flaring up again.

## Cautions
- The masterlist holds two conflicting records for FIR-002-50LBS-AFFF. One says AFFF 50 lb wheeled. The other says HCFC 50 lb wheeled. Confirm the agent and the price with CTI sales.
- AFFF has contained PFAS since the 1970s. PFAS do not break down naturally in the environment.
- Studies link some PFAS to harmful health effects in people and animals. Fluorine-free foams contain no PFAS.
- U.S. OSHA requires employers to train staff in extinguisher use when hired and at least once a year.
- In workplaces, U.S. OSHA requires a monthly visual inspection and an annual maintenance check. AFFF units need a hydrostatic test every 5 years.
