## Specs
- Product type: Exit sign, lighted, LED
- Faces: single faced (FIR-002-30A), double faced (FIR-002-30B)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. An exit sign marks an exit so that people can find it in an emergency. A lighted LED exit sign uses light-emitting diodes to light its face. In most commercial buildings, lighted exit signs operate 24 hours a day. U.S. OSHA requires a clearly visible sign that reads "Exit" at each exit in a workplace.

## Advantages
- A U.S. utility says LED signs use about 70% less energy than fluorescent signs.
- The same utility says LED signs use about 90% less energy than incandescent signs.
- LEDs have a long life and need little maintenance.
- Some LED exit signs have a battery backup. The battery keeps the exit lit during a power outage.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name does not state if CTI's unit has a battery backup. Ask CTI sales.
- Exit signs with a battery backup need inspection and testing every month.
- U.S. OSHA requires the word "Exit" in letters at least 6 inches high.
- U.S. OSHA requires the main strokes of those letters to be at least 3/4 inch wide.
- U.S. OSHA requires a reliable light source and a sign that is distinctive in colour.
- U.S. OSHA requires a clear line of sight to an exit sign at all times.
- UK workplace guidance requires regular checks that fire exit signs are in the right place.
