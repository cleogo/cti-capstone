## Specs
- Product type: Fire extinguishing ball
- Weight: 1.3 kg

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A fire extinguishing ball is a self-activating extinguisher that holds a dry chemical powder. You can throw or roll it into a fire. You can also mount it on a wall near a fire risk. Flame activates the ball, and it makes a loud noise when it activates.

## Advantages
- It needs no pin and no training to use.
- A mounted ball gives passive protection, so it can act on a fire with no one present.
- The loud noise on activation also works as an alert.

## Cautions
- A fire ball does not replace a fire extinguisher or a fire suppression system.
- The Bureau of Fire Protection said fire balls activate only when a fire is already big.
- The BFP does not advise fire balls for small fires. It promotes the fire extinguisher, which can put out a starting fire.
- In 2023, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission told consumers to stop using several brands of fire balls.
- The CPSC found that these balls can fail to spread their powder and fail to put out a fire.
