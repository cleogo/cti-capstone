## Specs
- Models: BLUETTI PV60, PV100, PV130, PV200, PV220, PV350 (CTI calls them Sora 60 to Sora 350)
- Rated power: 60 W (PV60), 100 W (PV100), 130 W (PV130), 200 W (PV200), 220 W (PV220), 350 W (PV350)
- Cell efficiency: 24.7% (PV60), 25% max (PV100 to PV350), per CTI's price list
- Ingress protection: IP65 (PV60, PV100), IP67 (PV130 to PV350), per CTI's price list

## Usage
Portable solar panels that charge a BLUETTI power station. Match the panels
to the station's solar input limits. CTI's solar guide recommends one Sora
220 for the Premium 30 V2 and Premium 50, two in series for the Premium 80
and Premium 150, three in series for the Premium 200, and four (two in
series on each port) for the APEX300.

## Advantages
- One range from 60 W to 350 W, so a panel fits each station size.
- Rated for outdoor use (IP65 or IP67).

## Cautions
- A panel delivers its rated power only in full sun. Real output is lower in
  clouds, heat and at poor angles.
- BLUETTI's own pages give some different figures: 23.4% efficiency and IP65
  for the PV350. CTI's price list is the figure shown here. Confirm with CTI
  sales if a rating matters.
- Never exceed the station's solar voltage limit when wiring panels in
  series.
