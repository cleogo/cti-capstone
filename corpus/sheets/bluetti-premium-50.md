## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 50 (AC50P)
- Capacity: 504 Wh
- Rated AC output: 700 W
- Power lifting: 1,200 W
- AC charging input: 600 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 200 W max, 12 to 28 V, 8.5 A
- Self-consumption: about 9 W
- Total outlets: 7

## Usage
Laptops, a router, a fan, a TV and lights through a short brownout, or a
camping trip. It charges from a wall socket or from solar. CTI's solar guide
pairs it with one Sora 220 (PV220) panel.

## Advantages
- About 500 Wh in a portable unit.
- 600 W Turbo AC charging refills it quickly from the wall.

## Cautions
- The manufacturer pages state no surge figure. Do not plan on starting a
  motor load above 700 W.
- The manuals give about 9 W self-consumption. The product page FAQs say 10 W.
