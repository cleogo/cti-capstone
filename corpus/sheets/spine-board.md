## Specs
- Product type: Spine board

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A spine board is also called a long backboard or spineboard. It is a rigid, full-length board. Rescuers use it with a cervical collar and a head immobilizer to limit the movement of a patient with a possible spinal injury. The Hong Kong Hospital Authority says that a spinal board is used to extricate a trauma patient and transfer the patient from the scene to hospital. Safe use needs training. One maker, Laerdal, says that spinal immobilization devices should only be used by personnel who have received adequate training. The masterlist does not state the material, size, load rating or straps of CTI's board. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- U.S. NAEMSP and ACS-COT call the backboard a useful spinal protection tool during extrication.
- U.S. NAEMSP advises a long spine board, a scoop stretcher or a vacuum mattress to help with patient transfers. Transfer is one of the riskiest times for more injury to the spine.
- Some boards let the patient stay on the board for X-ray, CT and MRI. The masterlist does not state this for CTI's board.

## Cautions
- Use a spine board only with enough trained people. The Hong Kong Hospital Authority asks for an adequate number of trained personnel to keep the whole spine in a neutral position during a logroll or a transfer.
- Use a spine board only when local protocol calls for it.
- U.S. NAEMSP and ACS-COT say that backboards can cause respiratory compromise, pain and pressure sores. They advise judicious use.
- Remove the patient from the board as soon as it is clinically indicated. The Hong Kong Hospital Authority recommends removal after the primary survey at hospital, or once the patient is stable.
- The Hong Kong Hospital Authority says that a spinal board has no role in penetrating trauma.
- Secure the patient with the straps. Secure the head last, with a head immobilization device.
- Inspect the board before each use. Stop using a board that has cracks, punctures or broken pins.
- The masterlist does not state if CTI's board comes with straps or a head immobilizer. Ask CTI sales.
