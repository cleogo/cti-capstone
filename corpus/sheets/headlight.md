## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. The masterlist lists this item with flashlights. A headlight of this kind is a light that you wear on your head, also called a headlamp. It keeps both hands free for work, climbing or carrying. U.S. NPS says that lighting helps you find your way at night and signal for help. The masterlist states only the name. It does not state the brightness, the runtime, the battery type or the strap type of CTI's unit. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- U.S. NPS recommends headlamps as a light source because they are hands-free.
- The light points where the wearer looks.
- U.S. Ready.gov lists a flashlight in a basic disaster supplies kit. A headlamp can do the same job.

## Cautions
- The masterlist does not state the battery type or the runtime. Ask CTI sales before you buy for a long task.
- U.S. NPS says to pack extra batteries.
- Test the light and replace the batteries before you store it in an emergency kit.
- U.S. USFA says to use flashlights, not candles, if you have a power outage.
- Do not point the light into the eyes of other people.
