## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A flashlight is a portable, hand-held light. U.S. Ready.gov lists a flashlight and extra batteries in a basic disaster supplies kit. It also says to have flashlights for every household member for a power outage. The masterlist states only the name. It does not state the brightness, the runtime or the battery type of CTI's unit. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- It gives light during a power outage, with no flame.
- U.S. USFA says to use flashlights, not candles, if you have a power outage.
- It is small enough to keep in a drawer, a vehicle or an emergency kit.

## Cautions
- The masterlist does not state the battery type or the runtime. Ask CTI sales.
- Keep extra batteries with the flashlight.
- Test the flashlight regularly. U.S. Ready.gov says to replace expired items in an emergency kit as needed.
- Do not point the light into the eyes of other people.
