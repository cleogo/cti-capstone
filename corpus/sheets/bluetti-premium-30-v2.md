## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 30 V2
- Capacity: 320 Wh
- Rated AC output: 600 W
- Surge power: 1,500 W
- Power lifting: 1,500 W
- AC charging input: 380 W
- Solar input: 200 W max, 12 to 28 V, 10 A
- Self-consumption: about 9 W (AC and DC on)
- Total outlets: 9

## Usage
Small loads for a few hours: laptops, a router, LED lights, a fan, a small
TV. It charges from a wall socket or from solar. CTI's solar guide pairs it
with one Sora 220 (PV220) panel.

## Advantages
- Twice the rated output of the Premium 20, in a small unit.
- 9 outlets for several small devices at once.

## Cautions
- Only the Australian spec table states a 1,500 W surge. The EU and UK pages
  give 1,500 W as lifting power only. Do not plan on a 1,500 W motor start.
- The 980 W figure on some pages is bypass power, not the charging rate.
