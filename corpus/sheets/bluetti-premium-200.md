## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 200 (AC200PL)
- Capacity: 2,304 Wh
- Rated AC output: 2,400 W
- Surge power: 7,200 W
- Power lifting: 3,600 W
- AC charging input: 800 W (Silent), 1,200 W (Standard), 2,400 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 1,200 W max, 12 to 145 V, 15 A
- Self-consumption: about 12 W
- Total outlets: 13
- Expansion: takes an expansion battery

## Usage
Home brownout backup with room to grow. It runs a refrigerator, fans, lights
and a TV through a night. An expansion battery adds capacity. CTI's solar
guide pairs it with three Sora 220 (PV220) panels in series.

## Advantages
- The widest solar voltage range in the Premium line, up to 145 V.
- Expandable. CTI's price list says the B300K battery (PWR-004-01) fits it,
  with the P090D-P150D cable (PWR-004-02).
- 13 outlets.

## Cautions
- Only the UK spec table gives a 7,200 W surge. The EU manual gives overload
  limits instead: 2,520 to 3,000 W for 2 minutes, 3,000 to 3,600 W for 5
  seconds. Plan on those.
- The UK page also lists 950 W Turbo charging, which conflicts with its own
  2,400 W figure.
