## Specs
- Product type: LED siren (as named on the masterlist)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. The masterlist name suggests a sounder with an LED light. A sounder beacon combines a sounder with a light. One maker's sounder beacon uses LED technology. Its sound is the primary alarm, and its light is a supplementary indication. UK guidance says sounders alert the occupants of a building.

## Advantages
- One device can give both sound and light.
- One maker says its LED sounder beacon gives this sound and light at a very low current draw.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name does not state the sound level, the tones, the light colour or the voltage of CTI's unit.
- Ask CTI sales if CTI's unit gives sound, light or both.
- Not every light on a sounder is a visual alarm device. UK maker Apollo says EN 54-23 covers lights that are the primary warning.
- Apollo says other lights are auxiliary indicators only. Do not use such a light as the only warning for people who cannot hear.
- U.S. OSHA says an employee alarm must be perceivable above the ambient noise or light by all employees.
- U.S. OSHA says the alarm must be distinctive and recognizable as a signal to evacuate.
- UK guidance says the warning signal and the sound level must suit the premises.
