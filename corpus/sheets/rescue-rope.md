## Specs
- Product type: Rescue rope
- Lengths: 10 m to 200 m (12 codes)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A rescue rope is used to reach, lower or pull a person in a rescue. In a water rescue, a person on the bank throws a line to the person in the water and pulls them to the side. UK Cheshire Fire and Rescue Service advises that you never enter the water yourself, no matter what. Safe rope rescue needs training. The masterlist does not state the material, diameter, construction or strength of CTI's rope, or if it floats. It also does not state if the rope is certified as life safety rope. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- A thrown line lets a rescuer help a person in the water from the bank. UK Cheshire Fire and Rescue Service says that a throw line is better than a floating object, if one is available.
- The masterlist has 12 lengths from 10 m to 200 m. Choose the length that suits the distance or height of the task.

## Cautions
- A rescue rope is not a fall-arrest line or a life safety line unless its maker rates it so. Read the rope's label and the maker's instructions.
- Washington State, U.S., requires that ropes which support the weight of firefighters or other persons be life safety rope that meets NFPA 1983.
- U.S. OSHA requires that fall protection lifelines be used only for employee fall protection and not for any other purpose, such as hoisting materials.
- Washington State, U.S., requires fire departments to keep written procedures for rope rescue and to use qualified inspectors for life safety rope.
- Inspect the rope before and after each use.
- Remove a rope from service if it has heat, flame, chemical or abrasion damage, or if it has held an impact load. If you doubt that a rope is safe, take it out of service.
- Pad the rope where it runs over edges or rough surfaces.
- Keep ropes away from chemicals. Sterling Rope says that even the fumes can damage the fibres, and that the damage can be invisible.
- Sterling Rope advises to retire a rope at once after exposure to excessive UV light, sharp edges or chemical contamination.
- Wash a rope in mild soap and water and hang it to dry in the shade. Do not put it in a dryer.
