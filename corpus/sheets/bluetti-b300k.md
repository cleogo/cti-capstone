## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI B300K Expansion Battery
- Capacity: 2,764.8 Wh
- Battery: LiFePO4, 51.2 V, 54 Ah
- Weight: 29.5 kg
- Dimensions: 525 × 327 × 209 mm

## Usage
Adds capacity to a power station. It has no outlets of its own, so it does
nothing without a compatible station. Connect it with the P090D-P150D
battery cable (PWR-004-02).

## Advantages
- Adds 2,764.8 Wh, the full capacity of an APEX300, to a compatible station.
- Lets a home grow its backup without replacing the station.

## Cautions
- BLUETTI sells the B300K in two variants: one for the APEX300, one for the
  AC200L, AC200MAX, AC300 and AC500. CTI's price list says it fits the
  Premium 200, AC300, AC500 and APEX300. Confirm the variant with CTI sales
  before ordering.
- At 29.5 kg it needs two people or a trolley to move.
