## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 100 V2
- Capacity: 1,024 Wh
- Rated AC output: 2,000 W
- Surge power: 3,600 W
- Power lifting: 2,700 W
- AC charging input: 1,200 W
- Solar input: 1,000 W max, 12 to 60 V, 20 A
- Self-consumption: about 10 W
- Total outlets: 11

## Usage
High-power loads for a short time: power tools, a kettle, a microwave, or a
refrigerator through a brownout. It also suits a site or a home that needs
2,000 W but not a large battery.

## Advantages
- 2,000 W rated output and a 3,600 W surge in a 1 kWh unit.
- Solar input of up to 1,000 W, twice that of the Premium 150.

## Cautions
- Capacity is 1,024 Wh. A 2,000 W load empties it in well under an hour.
- The 2,300 W figure on the pages is AC input with bypass, not the charging
  rate.
