## Specs
- Product type: Fire hose cabinet
- Parts named on the masterlist: angle valve, lock nipple, straight nozzle or polycarbonate nozzle
- Hose size: 1 1/2 in
- Hose length and jacket: 50 ft single jacket (FIR-002-38A), 50 ft double jacket (FIR-002-38B), 100 ft single jacket (FIR-002-38C), 100 ft double jacket (FIR-002-38D)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A fire hose cabinet holds a hose, a valve and a nozzle at a standpipe outlet. U.S. OSHA requires hose cabinets to permit prompt use of the hose and the valve. A single jacket hose has one woven polyester jacket around a rubber lining. A double jacket hose has a second polyester jacket.

## Advantages
- One U.S. supplier recommends single jacket hose for occupant-use standpipe systems.
- A double jacket hose holds much higher pressure than a single jacket hose.
- A double jacket hose also resists the elements better than a single jacket hose.
- A 1 1/2 in hose is about the largest size that one person can control when it is full of water.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name does not state the cabinet material, the cabinet size or the hose pressure rating. Ask CTI sales.
- U.S. OSHA requires standpipe hose to have a shut-off type nozzle.
- U.S. OSHA requires hose cabinets to be clearly identified and used only for fire equipment.
- U.S. OSHA requires an inspection at least once a year and after each use.
- U.S. OSHA requires other protection, such as extinguishers, while a hose system is out of service.
- Clean a jacketed hose with a soft brush and mild soap. Then rinse it and dry it.
