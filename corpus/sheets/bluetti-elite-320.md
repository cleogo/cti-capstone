## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI ELITE320
- Capacity: 3,200 Wh
- Rated AC output: 1,800 W
- Surge power: 3,600 W
- Power lifting: 2,700 W
- AC charging input: 1,440 W
- Solar input: 1,000 W max, 12 to 60 V, 20 A
- Self-consumption: about 10 W
- AC outlets: 4 × 120 V / 15 A

## Usage
Long brownouts with moderate loads: a refrigerator, fans, a TV and lights
through a full night. It has the largest capacity in the range for its
output.

## Advantages
- 3,200 Wh, the largest single-unit capacity CTI sells.
- Low self-consumption, about 10 W, for its size.

## Cautions
- Rated output is 1,800 W, the same as the Premium 150. Capacity is large,
  but output is not. It does not run a large aircon.
- The AC outlets on CTI's price list are 120 V types. Confirm the outlet
  configuration of the unit you receive with CTI sales.
- No manual was checked. Specs come from the EU product page only.
