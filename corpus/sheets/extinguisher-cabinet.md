## Specs
- Product type: Fire extinguisher cabinet
- Sizes on the masterlist: for a 10 lb extinguisher (FIR-002-CAB10) and for a 20 lb extinguisher (FIR-002-CAB20)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of cabinet, not the datasheet of CTI's unit.
A cabinet gives a portable extinguisher a fixed place on the wall. In
workplaces, U.S. OSHA requires employers to mount, locate and identify extinguishers
so staff can reach them readily. OSHA also requires a used extinguisher to go
back to its designated place.

## Advantages
- A fixed cabinet gives staff one known place to find the extinguisher.
- A cabinet helps keep the extinguisher in its designated place between uses.

## Cautions
- Keep the cabinet front clear. Nothing should block or limit access to the extinguisher.
- A cabinet does not replace upkeep. U.S. OSHA requires a monthly visual inspection and an annual maintenance check.
- U.S. OSHA requires workplace extinguishers to stay fully charged and in working order.
- Pick the cabinet that matches the extinguisher size. Confirm the fit of a specific unit with CTI sales.
