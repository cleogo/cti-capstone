## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A fire blanket is fire-resistant fabric that smothers a small fire by cutting off its oxygen. Lay it over the fire to cover the flames, or wrap it around a person whose clothing is on fire. CTI sells the 1.2x1.2, 1.5x1.5 and 1.8x1.8 sizes in two product lines, FIR-001 and FIR-002. The two lines are different items, and each code has its own price on the masterlist.

## Advantages
- It needs no special training to use.
- It can put out a cooking fat fire.
- It can protect a person whose clothes catch fire.

## Cautions
- Use a fire blanket only on a small fire.
- Mount it near the kitchen door, not next to the stove. A stove fire can block your way to it.
- Call the fire service after you use a fire blanket, even if the fire is out.
- Replace a fire blanket after use. The European standard BS EN 1869 covers blankets that are not reusable.
- Inspect the blanket regularly to make sure that it is in good condition.
