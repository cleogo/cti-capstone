## Specs
- Product type: Portable fire extinguisher
- Agent: carbon dioxide (CO2)
- Sizes on the masterlist: 10 lb (from the code)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. CO2 extinguishers are designed for Class B and C fires only. Class B covers flammable liquids, and Class C covers energized electrical equipment. CO2 puts out a fire by displacing oxygen, and dry ice from the horn also cools it. You can recognize the type by its hard horn and the absence of a pressure gauge.

## Advantages
- CO2 does not conduct electricity.
- It discharges as a gas and leaves no residue.
- NFPA 10 advises carbon dioxide or halogenated extinguishers for delicate electronics such as computers.

## Cautions
- CO2 is not recommended for Class A fires. These fires may smolder and reignite after the CO2 dissipates.
- Never use a CO2 extinguisher in a confined space with people present unless they wear respiratory protection.
- Pieces of dry ice shoot from the horn during discharge.
