## Specs
- Product type: Luminous signage, glow in the dark
- Legends on the masterlist: Exit Sign Left (FIR-002-31A), Exit Sign Right (FIR-002-31B), Exit Sign Green (FIR-002-31C), Exit Sign Red (FIR-002-31D), Fire Exit (FIR-002-31E)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. The masterlist name does not state the luminous material. A photoluminescent sign absorbs energy from light in well-lit surroundings. It then releases that energy over time as a glow. U.S. OSHA requires a clearly visible sign that reads "Exit" at each exit in a workplace.

## Advantages
- A photoluminescent sign glows with no dedicated power source.
- It needs no wiring.
- It has no battery, so it needs no yearly battery test.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name does not state the glow duration or the sign size. Ask CTI sales.
- A photoluminescent sign needs a reliable light source to charge it.
- A U.S. supplier notes that lights on a timer cannot charge the sign if they switch off during occupied hours.
- U.S. OSHA accepts self-luminous signs with a surface luminance of at least 0.06 footlamberts.
- U.S. OSHA requires the word "Exit" in letters at least 6 inches high, with strokes at least 3/4 inch wide.
- U.S. OSHA requires an exit sign to be distinctive in colour.
- UK workplace guidance requires regular checks that fire exit signs are in the right place.
