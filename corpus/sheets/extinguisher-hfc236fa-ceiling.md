## Specs
- Product type: Ceiling-type fire extinguisher
- Agent: HFC-236fa
- Sizes on the masterlist: 10 lb, 20 lb
- Mounting: Ceiling type

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A ceiling-mounted automatic extinguisher detects fire with a thermal sensor and activates on its own. It fixes to the ceiling with a bracket. One maker offers ceiling-mounted units filled with HFC-236fa. HFC-236fa is a colorless, odorless clean agent that discharges as a liquid and flashes to a gas.

## Advantages
- An automatic ceiling unit activates in a fire by itself.
- It needs no piping or ducting, so installation is quick.
- HFC-236fa does not conduct electricity and leaves no residue.
- It does not cause thermal shock damage to delicate electronics.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name says ceiling type only. It does not state how CTI's unit activates.
- U.S. OSHA guidance on fixed gaseous systems warns that some agents stay at hazardous levels after discharge.
- U.S. OSHA says to ventilate a flooded area before people reenter without protective clothing and respirators.
- U.S. OSHA notes that some halogenated hydrocarbons decompose at high temperatures.
- The Kigali Amendment lists HFC-236fa as a controlled substance with a global warming potential of 9,810. Parties must cut HFC use in steps.
