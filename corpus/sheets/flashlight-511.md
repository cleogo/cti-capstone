## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. The masterlist states only the name "Flash LIGHT-511". It does not say if "511" is a brand, a model number or a size. Do not assume that it is a product of a brand with a similar name. A flashlight is a portable, hand-held light. U.S. CDC advises flashlights or other battery-powered lights instead of candles when the power is out. U.S. Ready.gov lists a flashlight in an earthquake supply kit. The masterlist does not state the brightness, the runtime or the battery type of CTI's unit. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- It gives light with no flame, so it is safer than a candle in a power outage.
- Be Ready Utah, a U.S. state preparedness site, says that a repeated series of three flashes calls for help at night.

## Cautions
- The masterlist does not state the maker or what "511" means. Ask CTI sales which product it is.
- The masterlist does not state the battery type or the runtime. Ask CTI sales.
- Keep extra batteries with the flashlight, and test it regularly.
- Do not point the light into the eyes of other people.
