## Specs
- Product type: Flash alarm (as named on the masterlist)
- Signal: Visual (flashing light)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A flash alarm gives a fire warning with a flashing light. UK maker Apollo calls this type a visual alarm device. A visual alarm device can be a separate unit. It can also be part of a sounder or a smoke detector. UK guidance says visual alarms can be necessary in noisy areas. They can also be necessary for people with hearing loss.

## Advantages
- It warns people who are deaf or hard of hearing.
- It warns people in high ambient noise and people who wear ear defenders.
- Scottish fire service guidance lists flashing lights among the alarms for people who cannot hear well.

## Cautions
- The masterlist name does not state the flash rate, the light colour or the voltage of CTI's unit.
- Apollo says EN 54-23 covers visual alarm devices that are the primary warning. These devices flash at 0.5 to 2 Hz.
- Apollo says other lights are auxiliary indicators only. They are not the primary means of alarm warning.
- U.S. OSHA says an employee alarm must be perceivable above the ambient noise or light by all employees.
- U.S. OSHA says tactile devices can alert employees who cannot recognize an audible or visual alarm.
- Scottish fire service guidance also lists vibrating pads for people who cannot hear well.
