## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 200 V2
- Capacity: 2,073.6 Wh
- Rated AC output: 2,700 W
- Power lifting: 4,050 W
- AC charging input: 1,200 W (Standard), 2,300 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 1,000 W max, 12 to 60 V, 20 A
- Self-consumption: about 10 W
- Total outlets: 9

## Usage
Whole-evening brownout backup for a home: refrigerator, fans, lights, TV and
router. It can also run a small inverter aircon for a limited time. Use the
recommender for the hours.

## Advantages
- About 2 kWh with 2,700 W rated output.
- Solar input of up to 1,000 W.

## Cautions
- No official page or manual states a surge figure. Do not plan on a motor
  start above 2,700 W.
- Turbo charging must be activated by BLUETTI support. At 220 V mains the
  manual lists 2,200 W max.
- Self-consumption is about 10 W in the EU manual. The UK page says 15 W.
