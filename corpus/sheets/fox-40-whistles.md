## Specs
- Brand: Fox 40
- Masterlist name (WHI-001-09): FOX 40 OLD
- Masterlist name (WHI-001-10): FOX 40 Hand Held
- Masterlist name (WHI-001-11): FOX 40 Stainess
- Fox 40 model: Not confirmed for any of the three codes. Ask CTI sales.

## Usage
Fox 40 makes whistles for referees, coaches, rescue and general safety. Its official range has more than 20 whistles, for example the Classic, Classic CMG, Mini, Sonik Blast CMG and Super Force CMG. It also has Fingergrip models and electronic whistles. The masterlist names do not match a Fox 40 model name. No source confirms which Fox 40 model each CTI code is. Ask CTI sales which model you will get before you rely on a spec.

## Advantages
- Fox 40 says that its pealess whistles have no moving parts, so they cannot jam as a pea whistle can.
- Fox 40 says that the Classic, its first pealess whistle, has a sound power of 115 dB and chambers that self-clear in water. These figures apply only if CTI's unit is a Classic.
- A Fox 40 mouth whistle needs no battery. Only the electronic models use batteries.

## Cautions
- "FOX 40 OLD" does not map to a Fox 40 model name. It can be an older design, but no source confirms this. Ask CTI sales.
- "FOX 40 Hand Held" does not map to one Fox 40 model. Fox 40 sells Fingergrip whistles and electronic push-button whistles. Ask CTI sales which one it is.
- "FOX 40 Stainess" can mean stainless steel. The official Fox 40 whistle range shows no stainless steel whistle. Its only metal whistle on the range page is the Titan, which Fox 40 describes as titanium. Ask CTI sales what this item is.
- Sound levels are different for each model. For example, Fox 40 states 115 dB for the Classic and 90 dB for the Titan. Do not use the figure of one model for another.
- Do not blow a whistle close to the ear of another person.
