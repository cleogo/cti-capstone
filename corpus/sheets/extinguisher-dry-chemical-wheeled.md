## Specs
- Product type: Wheeled fire extinguisher
- Agent: Dry chemical
- Sizes on the masterlist: 50, 100 and 200 lb
- Mounting: FIR-002 units are wheeled type. FIR-001 names do not state a mounting.

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of extinguisher, not the datasheet of CTI's unit.
A wheeled unit holds a large volume of agent for high hazard areas. Makers
list uses such as airports, loading docks, fuel storage, spray paint booths
and heavy manufacturing. Makers offer wheeled units with different dry
chemicals, such as ABC and Purple K. The masterlist name does not say which
agent the unit holds, so read the label for its fire classes.

## Advantages
- Large agent capacity suits extra high hazard areas.
- Makers design these units so that one person can move and operate them.
- Some models use wide steel wheels to move over sandy or soft ground.

## Cautions
- U.S. OSHA requires employers to train staff in extinguisher use when hired and at least once a year.
- Use an extinguisher only on a small, contained fire. Make sure you have a clear escape route.
- Check the gauge, and look for dents, rust or a damaged hose or nozzle.
- In workplaces, U.S. OSHA requires a monthly visual inspection and an annual maintenance check.
- U.S. OSHA requires a hydrostatic test every 12 years for stored-pressure dry chemical units with mild steel shells. Owners must also empty and service these units every 6 years.
