## Specs
- Product type: Rope ladder
- Length: 10 m

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. A portable fire escape ladder gives an alternate escape route from an upper-floor window. U.S. NFPA recommends one ladder in every occupied room on floors above the main level. The masterlist does not state how CTI's ladder attaches to a window or its load rating. Ask CTI sales.

## Advantages
- It gives a second way out when flames block the door.
- Many escape ladders are small enough to store in a closet or under a bed.

## Cautions
- Store the ladder in or near the room where people can need it.
- Make sure that the window opens fully and that no furniture blocks it.
- Use the ladder only with the window type and floor height that it is designed for.
- Lower the ladder fully before anyone starts to descend.
- Do not overload the ladder. Use it only for its intended purpose.
- Inspect the ladder for damage or missing parts.
- Do not descend from an upper window only for a practice drill.
