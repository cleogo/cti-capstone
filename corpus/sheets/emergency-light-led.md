## Specs
- Product type: Emergency light, LED
- Voltage: 6 V (FIR-002-20A), 12 V (FIR-002-20B)

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. An emergency light comes on by itself when the power to the normal lighting fails. It lights escape routes and exits so that people can leave a building. The masterlist states only the voltage, so ask CTI sales for the runtime and the battery details.

## Advantages
- It switches on when the power fails, with no action from anyone.
- It helps keep exit routes lit so that people can see their way out.

## Cautions
- UK fire guidance sets a minimum duration of one hour for an emergency escape lighting system.
- Premises that people do not leave at once, such as sleeping accommodation, need at least three hours.
- UK workplace rules require regular checks that the emergency lighting works, and a record of any faults.
- Keep a log of all system tests and their results.
- In U.S. workplaces, U.S. OSHA requires each exit route to have enough light to see along it.
