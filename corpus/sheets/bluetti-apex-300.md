## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI APEX300
- Capacity: 2,764.8 Wh
- Rated AC output: 3,840 W
- Surge power: 7,680 W
- Power lifting: 7,680 W
- AC charging input: 500 W (Silent), 1,440 W (Standard), 3,840 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 2,400 W max (2 ports, 1,200 W each), 12 to 60 V, 20 A per port
- Self-consumption: about 20 W
- AC outlets: 4 × 120 V / 20 A, TT-30R, L14-50R
- Expansion: takes B300K expansion batteries

## Usage
Home battery backup for heavy loads: aircon, refrigerator and pumps. It is
the highest-output station CTI sells. CTI's solar guide pairs it with four
Sora 220 (PV220) panels, two in series on each port.

## Advantages
- 3,840 W rated output.
- Two solar ports, 2,400 W total.
- Expandable with the B300K battery (PWR-004-01) for more capacity.

## Cautions
- The 7,680 W surge comes from page text. The manual gives the same number as
  power lifting. A BLUETTI forum post quotes tiered overload limits instead.
  Plan on 3,840 W continuous.
- The AC outlets on CTI's price list are 120 V types. Confirm the outlet
  configuration of the unit you receive with CTI sales.
- Self-consumption is about 20 W, the highest in the range.
