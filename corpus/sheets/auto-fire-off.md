## Specs
- Product type: Self-activating fire extinguishing ball

## Usage
These are general facts for this type of product, not the datasheet of CTI's unit. Auto Fire Off, or AFO, is a name used on fire extinguishing balls. An AFO ball is a self-activating extinguisher that holds a dry chemical powder. You can throw or roll it into a fire, or mount it on a wall. The masterlist states no size or weight, so ask CTI sales for these details.

## Advantages
- It needs no pin and no training to use.
- You can mount it for passive protection, or throw it at a fire.
- Some AFO balls come with a stand that mounts to a wall.

## Cautions
- In 2023, the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission told consumers to stop using some balls labelled AFO Auto Fire Off.
- The CPSC found that these balls can fail to spread their chemicals and fail to put out a fire.
- The CPSC found that these balls do not meet NFPA 10, the U.S. standard for portable fire extinguishers.
- In a U.S. fire department test, an AFO ball put out part of a dumpster fire but not all of it.
- The AFO maker does not generally advise throwing the ball at a flammable liquid (Class B) fire.
