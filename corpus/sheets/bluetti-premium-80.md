## Specs
- Model: BLUETTI Premium 80 (AC70P)
- Capacity: 864 Wh
- Rated AC output: 1,000 W
- Surge power: 1,500 W
- Power lifting: 2,000 W
- AC charging input: 950 W (Turbo)
- Solar input: 500 W max, 12 to 58 V, 10 A
- Self-consumption: about 15 W
- Total outlets: 8

## Usage
A home brownout kit for fans, lights, a TV, a router and a small
refrigerator. CTI's solar guide pairs it with two Sora 220 (PV220) panels
in series.

## Advantages
- 1,000 W rated output runs most household loads except aircon and cooking
  appliances.
- Solar input of up to 500 W.

## Cautions
- The UK spec table gives 950 W AC charging. The EU manual V1.0 gives 850 W.
- Self-consumption is about 15 W, so long runs at low load use a large part
  of the battery for the station itself.
