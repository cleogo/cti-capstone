# Bluetti data

Generated by `python scripts/clean_bluetti.py` from the files supplied in
`data/raw/`: `bluetti-category.csv`, `bluetti-leyte-calculator.xlsx`,
`bluetti-prices.csv` (CTI's retail prices, SRP) and `bluetti-specs.csv`.
Do not hand-edit the CSVs here. Fix the source and re-run.

| File | Rows | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `products.csv` | 25 | Every Bluetti item. `rated_w` and `capacity_wh` parsed from the product name. Calculator columns filled for the 6 models the workbook covers. `price_php` (integer pesos, SRP, VAT inclusive, effective 01 Aug 2026) filled for 18 items, read from the two photographed price-list pages in `images/`. The other 7 are blank. Seven more columns come from the same photos via `bluetti-specs.csv`: `ac_output_w`, `power_lifting_w`, `total_outlets` (Premium line), `ac_outlets` (ELITE320, APEX300), `warranty_months`, `cell_efficiency_pct` and `ingress_protection` (solar panels). The script checks the photo's capacity and AC output against the figures parsed from the name. |
| `appliances.csv` | 183 | The WATTAGE sheet, deduplicated across user types. One row per appliance and variant, with `running_w` and `surge_w` side by side. `user_types` lists where the appliance appears (Residential, Office, Café / Restaurant, …). |
| `solar.csv` | 7 | Solar input limits and recommended panel wiring per model. |

## The calculator's formulas (Formula Reference sheet)

```
usable energy (Wh)   = capacity × DOD × inverter efficiency
total load (W)       = Σ(appliance watts × qty) + station self-consumption
runtime (h)          = usable energy ÷ total load
charging time (h)    = capacity ÷ charging power + trickle time
```

Defaults: DOD 0.90, inverter efficiency 0.85, trickle time 0.5 h (0.75 and 1.0
are the other options). Check: APEX 300 with one 150 W fridge gives
2764 × 0.9 × 0.85 ÷ (150 + 20) = 12.438 h, which matches the workbook. The
script asserts this.

## Issues found in the source workbook

1. **The dropdown uses surge watts as running watts.** "Air Conditioner (1.0 HP
   non-inverter split-type)" looks up 1,800 W. The WATTAGE sheet says 1,800 W
   is the 5-second start-up and 1,300 W is running. Runtime for that aircon is
   understated by about 28%. `appliances.csv` keeps the two values apart.
2. **RUNNING TIME PER APPLIANCES ignores DOD, efficiency and self-consumption.**
   It divides raw capacity by watts. For a 150 W fridge on the APEX 300 it
   shows 18 h 26 min. The Runtime Calculator says 12 h 26 min. The two sheets
   disagree by about 30%. Not exported. The app computes runtime with the
   formulas above.
3. **The calculator never checks output power.** A 1,800 W load on the Premium
   30 V2 (600 W rated) still gets a runtime. The app must reject a load above
   `rated_w`.
4. **APEX 300 AC charging is 12,000 W.** A standard 230 V / 15 A outlet
   supplies about 3,450 W, so a 0.73 h charge time is not reachable from a wall
   socket. Confirm the figure with CTI.
5. **APEX 300 capacity:** the calculator uses 2,764 Wh, the product name says
   2,764.8 Wh. Harmless (0.03%), noted in `products.csv`.
6. **Only one appliance has a surge value** (the non-inverter aircon). Fridges,
   freezers, pumps and power tools also surge on start-up. A surge check needs
   these values, or a documented multiplier.
7. **Solar sheet names panels CTI doesn't list.** SOLAR PANEL RECOMMENDATION
   recommends "Sora 60/130/220". The product list has PV60 to PV350, no Sora.
   The price list has no Sora. It lists PV60, PV130 and PV220 at 8,099, 14,990
   and 23,980, the prices an earlier `bluetti-prices.csv` gave to Sora 60, 130
   and 220. Those rows now use the PV names. Confirm with CTI that Sora is the
   PV line renamed, or that the workbook is out of date.
8. The placeholder check in the appliance lookup compares against
   `"- Select -"` while the dropdown says `"-SELECT-"`. Harmless: the lookup
   falls back to blank anyway.

## Still missing for the recommender

- `price_php` for 7 items the price list does not cover: AC300, AC500, EP760,
  HV800, EP2000, B500K, B700.
- Calculator specs (self-consumption, charging power) for 7 power stations:
  Premium 20, Premium 100 V2, Premium 200 V2, ELITE320, AC300, AC500, EP760.
  AC300, AC500 and EP760 also have no capacity: they take expansion batteries.
- Surge (peak) output ratings for every station. The price list's "Power
  Lifting" figure (`power_lifting_w`) is not a surge rating: it is Bluetti's
  lifting mode, which runs resistive loads above the AC output at reduced
  voltage. Do not use it as a start-up surge limit.
- Specs for the 7 items the price list does not cover (AC300, AC500, EP760,
  HV800, EP2000, B500K, B700).
- PV220's cell efficiency and IP rating are partly hidden by glare in the photo.
  The values stored (25%, IP67) are inferred from the neighbouring panels.
